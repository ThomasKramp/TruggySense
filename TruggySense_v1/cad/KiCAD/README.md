## KiCAD

- **teensy_board**: First version of the electrical schematic where the PCBs are subdivided based on the constrained PCB size I had to make them at home.
- **teensy_board_v2**: A V2 schematic which has an optimized schematic. This could be a good starting point to develop a new PCB.
- **simulation_io_protection**: Simulation in KiCAD to simulate the IO protection.
- **simulation_current_protection**: Simulation in KiCAD to simulate the current protection.
- **simulation_battery_stabilizer**: Simulation in KiCAD to simulate the battery stabilization.
- **simulation_adc_protection**: Simulation in KiCAD to simulate the ADC protection.
- **out_protection**: Sample PCB of an optocoupler to test the circuit.
- **battery_connection**: Schematic and PCB to distribute the voltage.
- **adc_buffer**: Sample PCB of an ADC buffer to test the circuit.
- **adc_buffer_v2**: Sample PCB of an ADC V2 buffer to test the circuit.
