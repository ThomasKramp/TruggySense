## KiCAD

- **1_logic_board**:        The board-design and electrical schematic that contains the LLC, most sensors and connector points.
- **2_power_board**:        The board-design and electrical schematic that contains the connection to the battery and provides power to the motors.
- **sensor_position**:      The board-design and electrical schematic of the GNSS/GPS module.
- **sensor_temperature**:   The board-design and electrical schematic of the temperature sensor.

- **io_protection_buffer**: An electrical schematic of the protection buffer, for certain input purposes, used in the logic board.
- **io_protection_input**:  An electrical schematic for the input protection, used in the logic board.
- **io_protection_output**: An electrical schematic for the output protection, used in the logic board.

- **layout_nrf24l01**:      A generic board-layout for nRF24L01 modules, used in the logic board.
- **layout_pim448**:        A generic board-layout for motion sensor, used in the logic board.

- **recreation_ina219**:    A recreation of both the electrical schematic and board-design, for the low-current sensor.
- **recreation_lm2596**:    A recreation of both the electrical schematic and board-design, for the voltage converter (5V --> 3V3).
