# TruggySense: Hardware and Firmware Redesign for Reproducibility

The continuation of the [TruggySense](https://github.com/Cosys-Lab/TruggySense), that was  originally developed by Robbe Elsermans, a new off-road era of (autonomous) RC-vehicle.

Further developed by Kramp Thomas through Cosys-Lab at University of Antwerp.

Welcome to the readme! A list of what this repo includes

- all files related to the end product,
- source code,
- manuals of the final system.

## cad
Everything related to specialized mounts, holders, and cases to build upon the Team Corally Kagama 1/8 RC vehicle chassis. 
Furthermore, it contains also schematics and circuits drawn in KiCAD.
